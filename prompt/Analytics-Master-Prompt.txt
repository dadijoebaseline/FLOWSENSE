CORE METRICS & CALCULATIONS
================================================================================

TOTAL METRICS (Dataset-Wide):

  Consumption Totals:
    Total Consumption (all)         = SUM(cumUsed) across all accounts, months
    Total Consumption (ACTIVE)      = SUM(cumUsed) where status="ACTIVE"
    Total Consumption (DISCONNECTED)= SUM(cumUsed) where status="DISCONNECTED"

  Revenue Totals:
    Total Revenue (all)         = SUM(billAmount) across all accounts
    Total Revenue (ACTIVE)      = SUM(billAmount) where status="ACTIVE"
    Total Revenue (DISCONNECTED)= SUM(billAmount) where status="DISCONNECTED"

  Account Counts:
    Total Active Accounts       = COUNT(distinct) where status="ACTIVE"
    Total Disconnected Accounts = COUNT(distinct) where status="DISCONNECTED"
    Total Accounts              = Active + Disconnected + Others


AVERAGE METRICS:

  Per-Account Averages:
    Avg Consumption/Account     = Total Consumption / Total Accounts
    Avg Revenue/Account         = Total Revenue / Total Accounts
    Avg Consumption (ACTIVE)    = Total Consumption(ACTIVE) / Active Accounts

  Temporal Averages:
    Avg Monthly Consumption     = Total Consumption / 4 months
    Avg Monthly Revenue         = Total Revenue / 4 months


DISTRIBUTION METRICS:

  Status Distribution:
    % ACTIVE        = (ACTIVE accounts / Total accounts) × 100
    % DISCONNECTED  = (DISCONNECTED accounts / Total accounts) × 100

  Consumption Distribution:
    Min Consumption = MIN(cumUsed)
    Max Consumption = MAX(cumUsed)
    Median Consumption = MEDIAN(cumUsed)
    Q1 (25th percentile)
    Q3 (75th percentile)

  Revenue Distribution:
    Min Revenue, Max Revenue, Median Revenue
    Q1, Q3 percentiles


DIMENSIONAL BREAKDOWN:

  By Area (area field):
    - Account count (total, ACTIVE, DISCONNECTED)
    - Total consumption and % of total dataset
    - Total revenue and % of total dataset
    - Average consumption per account in area
    - Status distribution (% ACTIVE in area)
    - Classification distribution (% Residential, Commercial in area)

  By Route (bookNo field):
    - Account count
    - Total consumption
    - Total revenue
    - Average consumption per account
    - Route efficiency = Revenue / Consumption
    - Peak consumption month
    - Status breakdown

  By Classification (rateCode field):
    - Account count
    - Total consumption (individual + category)
    - Total revenue
    - Average consumption per account
    - Consumption trend (month-over-month)
    - Revenue per unit comparison to other classifications

  By Status:
    For ACTIVE:
      - Count, % of total
      - Total consumption, avg/account
      - Total revenue, avg/account
      - Status trend (becoming active vs staying)
    
    For DISCONNECTED:
      - Count, % of total
      - Why disconnected (if available)
      - Reconnection potential
    
    For NEW (if applicable):
      - Count
      - Initial consumption patterns
      - Classification distribution


TEMPORAL METRICS:

  Month-over-Month:
    Feb→Mar: Growth Rate = ((Mar - Feb) / Feb) × 100 %
    Mar→Apr: Growth Rate
    Apr→May: Growth Rate
    
    Per dimension: Show which gained/lost, which > 10% change

  Year-over-Year (if multiple years available):
    Same month, different years
    Growth rate comparison

  Seasonal Patterns:
    Average consumption by month
    Identify peak/low months
    Account for data limitations (Feb-May only)

================================================================================
COMPARATIVE ANALYSIS: ALL ACCOUNTS vs ANOMALIES
================================================================================

KEY INSIGHT: Contextualize anomalies as "% of dataset"

Anomaly Impact Analysis:
  Total Accounts              = A
  Accounts with Anomalies     = B
  Anomaly Impact %            = (B / A) × 100 %
  
  Display: "2,150 total accounts | 47 with anomalies (2.2%)"

Consumption Comparison:
  Total Dataset Consumption       = C
  Anomalous Account Consumption   = D
  Normal Account Consumption      = C - D
  
  % Normal = ((C - D) / C) × 100 %
  % Anomalous = (D / C) × 100 %
  
  Display: "Normal accounts: 94% of consumption | Anomalous: 6%"

Revenue Comparison:
  Total Revenue               = R
  Revenue from anomalous      = R_anom
  Revenue Loss/Risk           = R_anom
  
  % Revenue Impact = (R_anom / R) × 100 %

Status Patterns:
  % Anomalies in ACTIVE = (anomalous active / total active) × 100
  % Anomalies in DISCONNECTED = (anomalous disconnected / total disconnected) × 100
  
  Insight: If ACTIVE=3% anomalies, DISCONNECTED=0.5%
  → Problem more prevalent in active accounts

Classification Patterns:
  Anomaly Rate by Classification:
    - Residential: 1.5% of residential accounts
    - Commercial: 3.8% of commercial accounts
    - Industrial: 5.2% of industrial accounts
  
  Insight: Which classifications need attention?

================================================================================
MEANINGFUL INSIGHTS & CONTEXTUAL LOGIC
================================================================================

OPERATIONAL HEALTH INDICATORS:

  Healthy Dataset Baseline:
    Normal Range (IQR based):
      Min: Q1 (25th percentile)
      Target: Median
      Max: Q3 (75th percentile)
      Outliers: Beyond Q3 or below Q1
    
    Display: "Normal consumption: 15-45 m³/month (IQR) | Median: 28 m³/month"

  Account Health Score (Per Account):
    - Status: ACTIVE / DISCONNECTED / Other
    - Consumption vs local average (area/classification)
    - Revenue vs local average
    - Trend: Increasing, Stable, Decreasing
    - Anomaly risk: Yes/No

EFFICIENCY METRICS:

  Route Efficiency (Per Route):
    - Revenue per account = Total Revenue / Account Count
    - Consumption per account = Total Consumption / Account Count
    - Revenue per unit water = Total Revenue / Total Consumption
    - Account density
    - Peak efficiency = (Revenue/unit) × (Account Count)
    - Benchmark: Compare to district average

  Area Performance (Per Area):
    - Revenue generation ranking
    - Consumption profile vs other areas
    - Account concentration
    - Growth trend (month-over-month)
    - Status health (% ACTIVE)

TREND ANALYSIS:

  Consumption Trends:
    Dataset-wide: Feb→Mar +X%, Mar→Apr +Y%, Apr→May +Z%
    Per dimension: Which areas/routes/classifications growing/declining?

  Revenue Trends:
    Same as consumption, but for billAmount
    Divergence check: Consumption up, Revenue down = billing issue?

  Account Movement:
    New connections: Feb → May count
    Disconnections: Feb → May count
    Reconnections: DISCONNECTED → ACTIVE changes

RISK & OPPORTUNITY INDICATORS:

  Revenue at Risk:
    Disconnected accounts:
      - Count
      - Previous consumption (if available)
      - Potential revenue if reconnected
      - Months disconnected
    
    Example: "847 disconnected accounts worth ~$125K annual revenue"

  Growth Opportunity:
    - Areas with high account growth
    - Classifications with rising consumption
    - New connections trend
    
    Example: "Area X added 23 new accounts (highest) with 15% higher avg consumption"

  Operational Concerns:
    - Routes with high disconnection rates
    - Areas with declining revenue
    - Sudden consumption drops in formerly stable accounts
